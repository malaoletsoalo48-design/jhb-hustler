Founder & CEO

Lebogang Nakedi

Rooted in the heart of Diepkloof, Johannesburg, Lebogang Nakedi established THE JHB HUSTLER (PTY) LTD with a clear, unyielding vision: to build a premier streetwear and athleisure brand that celebrates authentic hustle, perseverance, and urban culture.

Guided by the powerful philosophy "If it has to be, Let It be", Lebogang has cultivated a movement that empowers the youth of South Africa to chase their goals with confidence, transforming everyday struggles into stepping stones of success.
